## 4. Ambiguous requests

Caller phrasings that DON'T map to a single service without
clarification. Each: what to ask before proceeding.

Example (from clinic vertical):
- "A follow-up" → follow-up to WHAT, WITH WHOM, WHEN was original visit
- "Regular check-up" → new patient exam vs adult recall exam vs pediatric
- "Cleaning" → which hygienist, deep cleaning vs regular, insurance-covered?

## 5. Real failure modes

What actually goes wrong in this vertical when receptionists (human
or AI) drop the ball. Ordered by frequency.

- Booked wrong service (e.g. gave 30min follow-up when patient needed 90min consultation)
- Booked with wrong provider (specialist required, generalist scheduled)
- Missed a required-info question (allergy, insurance eligibility, referral)
- Quoted wrong price (didn't check insurance coverage first)
- Missed an upsell / cross-sell that would've helped the caller
- Handled emergency wrongly (should've triaged out, tried to book)
